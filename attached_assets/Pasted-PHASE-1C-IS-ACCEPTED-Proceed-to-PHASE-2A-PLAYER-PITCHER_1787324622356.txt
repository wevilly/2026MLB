PHASE 1C IS ACCEPTED.

Proceed to:

PHASE 2A - PLAYER, PITCHER, PARK, AND STATCAST RESEARCH DATA FOUNDATION

Do NOT begin betting models, prediction probabilities, FIRE/HALF/HOLD picks, bettor intelligence, or the AI Analyst.

The objective of Phase 2A is to build the baseball research layer that future independent TB, XBH, Walk, and HR engines will consume.

This phase should answer:

- Who is this hitter?
- Who is this pitcher?
- What does each player actually do well or poorly?
- What pitch shapes and pitch types are involved?
- How have those skills changed over different time windows?
- What does the park do to singles, doubles, triples, and home runs?
- What data is reliable, stale, missing, or sample-size limited?

NO PREDICTION OUTPUTS YET.

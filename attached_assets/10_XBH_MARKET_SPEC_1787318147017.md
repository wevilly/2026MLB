# Extra Base Hit Market Specification

## Canonical market key

`EXTRA_BASE_HIT`

## Initial target

`1+ XBH` / `Over 0.5 Extra Base Hits`

## Settlement

HIT when:

`2B + 3B + HR >= 1`

Singles do not count.

## Important distinction from 2+ TB

2+ Total Bases can be satisfied by two singles.

1+ XBH cannot.

Therefore the two markets must not share the same player ranking.

## Future XBH hitter research fields

### Outcome components
- doubles
- triples
- home runs
- XBH
- 2B/PA
- 3B/PA
- HR/PA
- XBH/PA
- XBH per hit

### Contact/damage shape
- line-drive rate
- hard-hit rate
- barrel rate and barrel/PA
- sweet-spot rate
- average EV
- 90th-percentile EV
- maximum EV
- launch-angle distribution

### Directional shape
- pull rate
- pull-air rate
- opposite-field damage
- directional doubles/XBH if obtainable

### Baserunning contribution
- sprint speed
- triples conversion opportunity

### Opportunity
- lineup spot
- expected PA
- platoon split

### Matchup
- hitter performance versus actual pitch types
- velocity/movement families
- opposing pitcher extra-base-contact profile
- likely bullpen path

### Park
Use component factors:
- doubles
- triples
- HR
- handedness
- dimensions/wall/altitude context

Do not use overall run factor as the primary XBH factor.

## Route label

Each future XBH prediction should support a mechanism route:

- DOUBLE_ROUTE
- TRIPLE_ROUTE
- HOME_RUN_ROUTE
- MULTI_PATH

Store both predicted route and realized route in postmortems when possible.

## Do not create a fake heuristic now

During the current data-foundation phase, collect and preserve the fields required for future XBH modeling.

Do not generate an XBH probability merely to populate the UI.

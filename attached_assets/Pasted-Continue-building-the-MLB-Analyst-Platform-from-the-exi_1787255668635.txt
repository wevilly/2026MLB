# THE FOUR CORE HITTER MARKETS

The platform must now treat these as FOUR INDEPENDENT research and modeling problems:

1. **2+ Total Bases**
2. **Extra Base Hit**
3. **Batter Walk**
4. **Home Run**

Do not create one universal hitter score and reuse it across all markets.

A player can rank:

* #1 for Extra Base Hit
* #8 for 2+ Total Bases
* #30 for Walk
* #15 for Home Run

That is expected.

The system should identify the market that best fits the player's actual mechanism.

# EXTRA BASE HIT DEFINITION

For this project:

**An Extra Base Hit means the batter records at least one double, triple, or home run.**

Singles do NOT count.

XBH = 2B + 3B + HR.

The standard prediction target should initially be:

**1+ Extra Base Hit / Over 0.5 XBH**

Keep XBH completely separate from 2+ Total Bases.

A hitter can reach 2+ Total Bases with:

* two singles
* one double
* one triple
* one home run

But XBH requires:

* double
* triple
* or home run

That distinction is fundamental.

Do not allow the UI, models, reports, or AI Analyst to describe Total Bases as an extra-base-hit market.

# MARKET-SPECIFIC BASEBALL LOGIC

## 1. 2+ TOTAL BASES

The TB engine should emphasize TWO paths:

### Contact/volume path

* plate appearances
* batting-order position
* hit probability
* contact rate
* strikeout rate

* doubles production

* opposing pitcher contact-inducing profile

### Damage path

* SLG
* xSLG
* barrels per PA
* hard-hit rate
* 90th percentile exit velocity
* pitch-type matchup
* park environment

A contact-inducing weak pitcher can be excellent for TB.

A strikeout-heavy pitcher can suppress TB even if he allows loud contact when the ball is put in play.

Build XBH as its own independent engine.

The XBH engine should emphasize:

* doubles per PA

* gap power

* hard-hit rate

* park doubles factor
* park triples factor

* outfield dimensions
* wall height
* altitude
* opposing starter XBH/contact-damage profile
* bullpen XBH/contact-damage path
* lineup position / projected PA

XBH should NOT simply rank the biggest home-run hitters.

Doubles-heavy hitters can be elite XBH candidates even if their HR probability is modest.

Fast hitters in large outfields can gain value through triples.

Pull-power hitters can gain XBH probability in parks with short pull-side dimensions.

A player's XBH profile should identify the dominant route:

* DOUBLE ROUTE
* TRIPLE ROUTE
* HOME RUN ROUTE
* MULTI-PATH

Store that route as an explainable feature.

# UPDATED CONFIDENCE SYSTEM

Use:

* FIRE
* HALF
* HOLD
* PASS
* PARKED

These represent **prediction confidence and research completeness**, not betting value.

## FIRE

One of the strongest market-specific baseball predictions on the slate.

Requires:

* strong supporting mechanism
* high-quality data
* reliable opportunity
* no major unresolved conflict

## HALF

Good prediction with meaningful supporting evidence, but weaker than FIRE.

## HOLD

Interesting research candidate with one or more material concerns.

## PASS

Researched and no meaningful predictive edge.

## PARKED

A blocking question remains unresolved.

Examples:

* uncertain starter
* opener/bulk role unclear
* player identity conflict
* lineup conflict
* injury/scratch uncertainty
* stale critical source
